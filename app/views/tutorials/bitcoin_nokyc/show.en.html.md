> [!IMPORTANT]
> There are several ways to buy bitcoin. The best option depends on your priorities: privacy, simplicity, liquidity, and the level of autonomy you are looking for.

> [!WARNING]
> **Services requiring identity verification (KYC)** permanently associate your identity with your bitcoin purchases. This information may be retained by the service and shared with authorities upon legal request. If privacy is important to you, consider using peer-to-peer (P2P) alternatives instead.

## Peer-to-peer (P2P) solutions

P2P platforms allow you to buy bitcoin directly from other individuals, without relying on a traditional exchange. They generally offer greater privacy and autonomy, although ease of use and decentralization vary.

### Mobile

- **[Peach Bitcoin](https://peachbitcoin.com/)**: a mobile-first P2P marketplace that connects buyers and sellers directly, without mandatory KYC.
- **[RoboSats](https://learn.robosats.com/)**: a privacy-focused P2P marketplace accessible from both mobile and desktop through the Tor network. No account or identity verification is required.

### Desktop / Web

- **[Noones](https://noones.com/)**: a P2P marketplace offering a wide range of payment methods for buying bitcoin directly from other users.
- **[RetoSwap](https://retoswap.com/)**: an open-source, decentralized P2P marketplace designed for users seeking greater autonomy.
- **[Bisq](https://bisq.network/)**: a fully decentralized desktop application for buying and selling bitcoin. It offers a high level of privacy and censorship resistance, but is intended for more experienced users.

## Centralized services (KYC)

These services are generally easier to use and may offer better liquidity, but they usually require identity verification (Know Your Customer, or KYC), making your bitcoin purchases traceable.

- **[Pocket Bitcoin](https://pocketbitcoin.com/)**: a simple service for buying bitcoin with identity verification.
- **[ChangeNOW](https://changenow.io/)**: may allow bitcoin purchases using various payment methods, including bank cards, depending on local regulations and service availability.

There is no one-size-fits-all solution. Consider the trade-offs between convenience, privacy, and sovereignty before choosing how to acquire your bitcoin.
