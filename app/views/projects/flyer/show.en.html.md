## What's inside the guide?

The flyer answers the most common questions people have when discovering Bitcoin and Monero.

You'll learn:

- how to install a cryptocurrency wallet;
- how to securely back up your recovery phrase;
- where to acquire your first cryptocurrencies;
- how to send and receive Bitcoin and Monero payments;
- which tools are available for merchants;
- where to find additional resources to continue learning.

The goal is simple: help you get started quickly using well-established tools and best practices.

## Are you an individual?

The flyer walks you through the first essential steps:

1. Install a wallet on your smartphone.
2. Secure it by safely storing your recovery phrase.
3. Obtain your first bitcoins or moneros.
4. Make your first cryptocurrency payment.

No technical background is required—just curiosity and a willingness to learn.

## Are you a merchant?

The guide also explains how businesses can begin accepting cryptocurrency payments.

It covers topics such as:

- letting customers know you accept Bitcoin and Monero;
- receiving payments quickly and easily;
- keeping track of transactions for accounting purposes;
- joining the collective's network of participating merchants.

The objective is not to replace existing payment methods overnight, but to offer customers an additional payment option.

## Why Bitcoin and Monero?

The **Bank-Exit** collective promotes the use of decentralized digital currencies to encourage direct, peer-to-peer exchanges between individuals and businesses.

Depending on your needs, Bitcoin and Monero can complement traditional payment systems while providing greater financial autonomy and privacy.
