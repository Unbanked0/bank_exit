How can you introduce cryptocurrencies to someone who does not use them yet? How can you send a small amount of Bitcoin, Monero or June in the same simple way you would give cash or a gift voucher?

The [digital cheque](https://monero-map.org/cheque) project provides a practical answer to these questions by using a format everyone understands: a paper document containing a digital value that can be claimed by its recipient.

## Why create a cryptocurrency cheque?

Digital currencies often rely on technical concepts that can seem complicated for beginners: wallets, addresses, private keys, recovery phrases…

The cheque helps simplify this first experience.

The idea is to build a bridge between two worlds:

- the familiar world of physical exchanges;
- the world of decentralized digital currencies.

A cheque can be handed over in person, offered during an event, used as a gift, or serve as an introduction to Bitcoin, Monero or June.

## How does the cheque work?

The principle is simple:

1. Someone creates a cheque containing an amount of digital currency.
2. The cheque is printed or given to the recipient.
3. The recipient scans the provided information.
4. The funds are recovered using a compatible wallet.

The document contains the information needed to access the value associated with the cheque, while keeping a format that remains understandable to everyone.

## A tool to discover free and alternative currencies

The cheque is an educational tool that allows people to gradually discover:

- **Bitcoin**, a decentralized digital currency used worldwide;
- **Monero**, a digital currency focused on transaction privacy;
- **June (Ğ1)**, a free currency based on a monetary creation system managed by its members.

These projects share a common goal: exploring alternatives to traditional monetary systems and experimenting with new forms of exchange.

## Practical uses

The cheque can be used in many situations:

- giving someone their first cryptocurrency;
- organizing a workshop or introduction event;
- rewarding a contribution to a project;
- facilitating first exchanges between users;
- introducing free and decentralized currencies without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

## A simple approach to encourage adoption

One of the main challenges of digital currencies is accessibility.

The cheque addresses this challenge by offering an intuitive experience:

> Receiving digital value can begin with a gesture as simple as receiving a document.

This approach helps create a connection between experienced users and people discovering these new tools.

## An open project by the Bank-Exit collective

The Bitcoin, Monero and June cheque is part of the **Bank-Exit collective**'s approach: encouraging the practical use of alternative currencies in everyday life.

Alongside merchant maps, tutorials and other tools developed by the collective, this project helps make digital payments more visible, simpler and more accessible.
