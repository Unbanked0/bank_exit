> [!IMPORTANT]
> The **Bank-Exit collective local groups** allow people interested in Bitcoin, Monero, June, and alternative payment solutions to meet, exchange knowledge, and support each other locally. These groups operate informally and rely on the involvement of community members.

Discovering digital currencies can sometimes feel overwhelming when learning alone.

How do you install a wallet?  
How do you make your first payment?  
How can a business accept Bitcoin or Monero?  
Who can you discuss these topics with?

The [local groups](<%= local_groups_path %>) were created to answer these needs: enabling real-world meetings, sharing knowledge, and building a support network around free and alternative currencies.

[![Local groups illustration](<%= image_path('projects/local_groups.jpg') %>)](<%= local_groups_path %>)

## Why local groups?

Digital tools make it possible to communicate remotely, but face-to-face meetings remain essential for learning and sharing knowledge.

A local group allows you to:

- meet other users near you;
- ask questions and get help;
- participate in practical workshops;
- discover Bitcoin, Monero, or June in a friendly environment;
- support newcomers during their first steps.

The goal is to create a network of people who can share their experiences and knowledge.

## How do local groups work?

Each group is independent and organized by the members who participate in it.

Meetings can take different forms:

- informal discussions over a drink;
- introductory workshops;
- help with installing and setting up wallets;
- exchanges between individuals and merchants;
- presentations of projects related to digital currencies.

You do not need to be an expert to participate: groups welcome both beginners and experienced users.

## Where can you find a group near you?

The Collective provides a map to help you find existing local groups.

You can:

1. Browse the list of available groups.
2. Search for your region or city.
3. Join the associated discussion channel.
4. Take part in upcoming events and meetings.

Groups are mainly located in France, with communities also developing in other countries.

## Would you like to create a local group?

You can contribute by starting a new group in your area.

A few motivated people are enough to get started:

- choose a meeting place;
- invite interested people;
- share educational resources;
- organize opportunities for discussion and exchange.

The Collective can help connect participants and make your group visible to the wider community.

## A community built on mutual support

Local groups are an essential part of the **Bank-Exit collective** approach.

Beyond the digital tools developed by the Collective (merchant map, L'Épicerie Libre, tutorials, cheques, and other projects), they create direct connections between people.

The goal is to encourage the practical adoption of digital currencies through shared experiences, learning, and mutual support.

> [!TIP]
> Curious about Bitcoin, Monero, or June? Joining a local group is often the easiest way to meet users, ask questions, and learn at your own pace.
