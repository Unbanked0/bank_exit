## Available solutions to buy Bitcoin

Several solutions are available to acquire Bitcoin:

- **Pocket Bitcoin**, presented in this tutorial, allows you to easily buy Bitcoin through a bank transfer while keeping control of your wallet.
- **Peer-to-peer (P2P)** solutions allow you to buy Bitcoin directly from other individuals.
- More advanced solutions such as **Peach Bitcoin** or **Haveno** allow direct exchanges between users without relying on a centralized intermediary.

In this guide, we focus on using **Pocket Bitcoin**.

## Step 1 – Install Pocket Bitcoin

Download the **Pocket Bitcoin** application from your app store.

On Android, search for:

```
Pocket Bitcoin
```

The official application has a purple logo with a large "P".

If you previously used **Relai**, you can restore your existing wallet using your recovery phrase.

> [!NOTE]
> Your Bitcoin wallet remains the same: only the application used to access it changes.

## Step 2 – Create or restore a Bitcoin wallet

When launching Pocket Bitcoin for the first time, you have two options:

- restore an existing wallet;
- create a new Bitcoin wallet.

If you already have a Relai wallet, choose **Restore existing wallet** and use your recovery phrase.

Otherwise, choose **Create a wallet**.

The application will generate a recovery phrase consisting of **12 words**.

### Secure your recovery phrase

Your recovery phrase provides full access to your Bitcoin wallet.

It must be stored with the utmost care:

- write it down on paper;
- ideally, engrave it on a durable metal plate.

Never store your recovery phrase:

- in a computer file;
- in a cloud service;
- by email;
- as a screenshot.

> [!WARNING]
> If you lose your recovery phrase, you permanently lose access to your bitcoins.

### Verify your backup

Pocket Bitcoin will then ask you to confirm the words of your recovery phrase in the correct order.

This step ensures that your wallet backup has been properly recorded.

Once verification is complete, your Bitcoin wallet is created.

## Step 3 – Discover your Bitcoin wallet

Your wallet displays:

- your Bitcoin balance;
- the equivalent value in euros;
- send and receive functions.

You can create multiple wallets if needed.

The application menu also allows you to access your recovery phrase.

## Step 4 – Buy Bitcoin with Pocket Bitcoin

To buy Bitcoin:

1. Open Pocket Bitcoin.
2. Click the Bitcoin button or **Connect**.
3. Enter your email address.

A confirmation code will be sent to verify your address.

### Create a purchase order

Enter the amount in euros you would like to purchase.

For example:

```
€100 worth of Bitcoin
```

The application automatically calculates the corresponding amount of Bitcoin.

Then:

- enter the IBAN of the bank account you will use for the transfer;
- select the Bitcoin wallet that will receive the funds;
- create your purchase order.

## Step 5 – Make the bank transfer

Pocket Bitcoin provides:

- a Swiss bank IBAN;
- beneficiary information;
- a payment reference.

From your bank:

1. Add Pocket Bitcoin as a new beneficiary.
2. Make a transfer for the indicated amount.
3. Make sure to include the provided reference in the transfer description.

This reference allows your payment to be matched with your order.

Once the transfer is received, the bitcoins will automatically be sent to your wallet.

## Step 6 – Verify Bitcoin reception

After processing the bank transfer, Pocket Bitcoin sends the bitcoins to your wallet.

You will receive an email notification once the transaction has been completed.

Your Bitcoin receiving address remains visible in the application if needed.

## Using your bitcoins

Once your bitcoins are available, you can store them or use them as a payment method.

Here are some examples of services allowing you to use Bitcoin:

- **Bank-Exit**: find merchants accepting Bitcoin, Monero and June.
- **BTC Map**: find merchants accepting Bitcoin near you.
- **Yorypto**: buy computer hardware with Bitcoin or Monero.
- **Bitrefill**: buy gift cards with Bitcoin.
- **Bitgild** and **Celtic Gold**: buy gold or silver using Bitcoin or Monero.

Other services also allow indirect purchases, for example through specialized intermediaries to buy products on Amazon.

## Going further: reducing your dependence on banks

Bitcoin allows you to keep part of your wealth outside the traditional banking system.

However, it is important to keep in mind that Bitcoin relies on the Internet to operate.

Its network is decentralized: it does not depend on a single company and cannot be stopped by shutting down a particular service provider.

## Best practices

- Start with a small amount to become familiar with Bitcoin.
- Always keep your recovery phrase offline.
- Carefully verify addresses before sending funds.
- Never share your recovery words.
- Comply with the legal and tax obligations applicable in your country.
