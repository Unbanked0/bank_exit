> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Nostr** (_Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays_) is a decentralized network protocol that acts as a distributed social network. Anyone can run their own relay (server), helping to prevent censorship through the redundancy of published messages.

In this tutorial, we will use the **Snort** client, but the process is similar on most Nostr websites and applications.

Once your account has been created with Snort, you can switch to another application or website at any time by importing your account using the private key generated during setup.

Go to https://snort.social and click **Sign Up**.

The registration process will then begin through several steps described below.

Choose a username.

![Create account](<%= image_path("tutorials/nostr-social-network/2_create_account.jpg") %>)

![Choose picture](<%= image_path("tutorials/nostr-social-network/3_choose_picture.jpg") %>)

![Select interests](<%= image_path("tutorials/nostr-social-network/4_select_interests.jpg") %>)

## Your Account Belongs to You

> [!NOTE]
> You will notice that the website never asks for your email address or phone number.
>
> This is because Nostr is based on cryptography and does not require any personal information. Your account belongs entirely to you!

## Back Up Your Private Key

It is now time to back up your private key.

To do this, click **Export keys** from the settings.

> [!CAUTION]
> It is extremely important to write down your private key and/or mnemonic phrase. This is the only way to restore your account.
> If you lose them, you will no longer be able to access your Nostr messages and contacts.
>
> **Never share this key with anyone.**

## Publish Your First Message

It is now time to publish your first message.

Click the **New note** button in the bottom-left corner, enter your content in the popup window, and confirm.

> [!NOTE]
> From now on, you should consider that any content published on a Nostr relay may remain available permanently.
> Even though a deletion feature exists, there is no guarantee that relays will honor the request.
>
> Additionally, since a message can be republished on any relay, it becomes difficult to know where the message is stored.
> This is also one of Nostr's strengths.
