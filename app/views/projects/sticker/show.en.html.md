> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CryptoBox** is a tool developed in the spirit of the **Bank-Exit collective** to allow anyone to easily receive donations in decentralized digital currencies. It generates a customized payment banner that can be displayed on a website, shared online, or printed.

How can you receive financial support directly in cryptocurrency?

For many projects, associations, creators, or independent workers, accepting donations can require complex technical solutions. [CryptoBox](https://monero-map.org/crypto_box) offers a simple approach: create a digital donation box compatible with several free and decentralized currencies in just a few clicks.

The goal is to enable direct exchanges between contributors and recipients, without relying on a centralized payment platform.

[![CryptoBox illustration](<%= image_path('projects/sticker_monero.png') %>)](https://monero-map.org/crypto_box)

## What is CryptoBox?

CryptoBox is a cryptocurrency donation banner generator.

It allows you to create a visual containing the information needed to receive payments in:

- **Monero (XMR)**;
- **Bitcoin (BTC)**;
- **June Ğ1**.

The generated banner can then be used on a website, in an online publication, on a poster, or any other communication medium.

## What can CryptoBox be used for?

CryptoBox is designed for anyone who wants to receive direct contributions.

Some examples of use:

- supporting a content creator;
- receiving donations for an open-source project;
- funding an association or community initiative;
- allowing users to contribute easily;
- displaying a payment address during an event.

## How do you create your donation banner?

Creating a CryptoBox is simple:

1. Choose the currency or currencies you want to accept.
2. Enter your receiving addresses.
3. Customize the available options.
4. Generate your banner.
5. Share it on your website or print it.

Contributors can then scan or copy the displayed information to make a payment directly from their wallet.

## A simple tool to encourage direct exchanges

Traditional donation systems often rely on intermediaries that collect personal information, charge fees, or impose their own rules.

With CryptoBox, payments are made directly between the contributor and the recipient through the Bitcoin, Monero, or June networks.

This approach reflects the philosophy of the **Bank-Exit collective**:

- encouraging direct exchanges;
- promoting the practical use of digital currencies;
- preserving greater autonomy in transactions.
