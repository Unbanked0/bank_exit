## Add a Contact

Before you can send a message, you need to add a new contact.

Click **New Message**.

![Create a new message](<%= image_path("tutorials/messaging/4_new_message.jpg") %>)

You can then enter the recipient's public key (identifier).

![Add a contact](<%= image_path("tutorials/messaging/5_add_contact.jpg") %>)

## Send a Message

You can now send your first text message to your recipient or start a voice call.

![Sending the first message](<%= image_path("tutorials/messaging/6_send_message.jpg") %>)

## Back Up Your Private Key

Remember to write down your own private key on paper.

To do this, go to the application settings.

> [!CAUTION]
> It is very important to write down these words.
> If you lose them, you will not be able to recover your account or your previous conversations.
>
> Never share this key with anyone.
