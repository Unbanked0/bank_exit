## Why Use a Sovereign Wallet?

A non-custodial wallet means that you directly control your funds.

You own:

- your private keys;
- your recovery phrase;
- the ability to send your funds without authorization from an intermediary.

Conversely, leaving your cryptocurrencies on an exchange platform means that you depend on a third party.

## Installing Cake Wallet

### Download the Application

Go to the official website: [https://cakewallet.com](https://cakewallet.com)

Cake Wallet is available on several platforms:

- Android through Google Play;
- Android via APK file;
- F-Droid.

Android allows you to install the application independently from the Play Store using the APK file.

> [!WARNING]
> Avoid keeping significant funds in a wallet installed only through a closed ecosystem.

## Creating a New Wallet

Open Cake Wallet and:

1. Accept the terms of use.
2. Select **Create a new wallet**.
3. Set a PIN code.

This PIN code only protects the application on your phone.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use the same code as your phone's unlock code.

## Choosing Monero

Cake Wallet supports multiple cryptocurrencies.

For this tutorial:

1. Select **Monero (XMR)**.
2. Give your wallet a name.
3. Choose the backup format:

- **Legacy 25 words**;
- French language.

## Backing Up Your Wallet

Cake Wallet will display a 25-word recovery phrase.

These words allow you to restore your wallet:

- on a new phone;
- after losing or replacing your device.

## Important Rules

✅ Write these words down on paper.

Even better:

- use a durable metal backup plate;
- keep several copies in secure locations.

❌ Do not take screenshots.

❌ Do not store this phrase in the cloud.

❌ Never share these words.

Anyone who has this phrase has access to your wallet.

## Setting the Display Currency to Euros

By default, Cake Wallet may display amounts in dollars.

To switch to euros:

1. Open the settings.
2. Go to **Display**.
3. Select **EUR** as the currency.

## Receiving Monero

In Cake Wallet:

1. Click **Receive**.
2. Copy your Monero address.
3. Share it with the person who needs to send you XMR.

You can also use the QR code.

Both methods refer to the same address.

## Sending Monero

To send XMR:

1. Click **Send**.
2. Enter the recipient's address.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Optionally add a note.
5. Confirm the transaction.

You can scan a QR code directly to avoid errors.

### Transaction Fees

Monero transaction fees are generally very low.

Cake Wallet allows you to adjust the transaction priority level depending on your need for faster confirmation.

## Transaction Confirmation

A transaction appears quickly after being sent.

However:

- it must then be confirmed by the network;
- your balance may remain temporarily unavailable while synchronization is in progress.

Make sure Cake Wallet is properly synchronized before making a payment.

## Buying Monero with Euros

Cake Wallet allows you to buy Monero directly from the application.

### Process

1. Click **Buy**.
2. Choose the available provider.
3. Select the desired amount.
4. Complete the requested bank transfer.

Your Monero address is automatically used to receive the funds.

### About Purchases

Depending on the amount and the provider, an identity verification process may be required.

Fees generally range between 1% and 3%.

## Spending Your Monero

Monero can be used with some merchants.

To find businesses accepting cryptocurrencies:

- use Bank-Exit map;
- search for merchants near you.

If no businesses directly accept Bitcoin or Monero in your area, services such as Bitrefill allow you to use cryptocurrencies to purchase certain services and gift cards.

## Security Best Practices

To remain sovereign:

- always keep your recovery phrase offline;
- verify addresses before sending a transaction;
- keep your application updated;
- never use a wallet where you do not own the keys.

## Conclusion

In just a few steps, you now have:

- a personal Monero wallet;
- the ability to send and receive payments;
- a way to buy XMR;
- a solution to use your funds directly with certain merchants.

Financial sovereignty begins with controlling your own keys.
