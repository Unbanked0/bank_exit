> [!IMPORTANT]
> **VoxPopuli** is a participatory consultation and decision-making tool developed around the **LibreMarket** ecosystem. It allows community members to share their opinions, participate in collective choices, and contribute to the evolution of projects supported by the community.

In a community-driven project, users are not only consumers: they can also participate in the directions and decisions that shape the future of the ecosystem.

This is the purpose of [VoxPopuli](https://vote.libre-market.com): to provide a simple space where everyone can take part in collective decision-making.

[![VoxPopuli illustration](<%= image_path(@record.cover) %>)](https://vote.libre-market.com)

## Why a voting tool?

Decentralized projects rely on the involvement of their users.

Rather than concentrating all decisions in the hands of a few people, participatory voting makes it possible to:

- collect feedback from the community;
- bring new ideas forward;
- prioritize future developments;
- encourage transparency in decision-making.

VoxPopuli aims to bring users closer to the decisions that affect the tools they use.

## How does VoxPopuli work?

The principle is intentionally simple:

1. Browse the proposals submitted to the community.
2. Review the different options available.
3. Express your preference.
4. Take part in discussions around the results.

The goal is not only to count votes, but to create a space for discussion that helps build future developments collectively.

## What types of decisions can be submitted?

A community voting system can allow users to give their opinion on different topics:

- new features;
- project directions;
- user experience improvements;
- development priorities;
- ecosystem evolution.

Each consultation helps better understand the needs and expectations of the people who use or support the project.

## A different way to build digital tools

Traditional platforms are often designed with a top-down approach: a company makes decisions, then users adopt them.

Open and community-driven projects offer a different approach:

- users can become active participants;
- decisions can be discussed collectively;
- tools evolve together with their community.

This philosophy aligns with the goal of the **Bank-Exit collective**: developing digital alternatives that promote greater autonomy, transparency, and participation.

## Taking part in the evolution of the ecosystem

By sharing your opinion, you directly contribute to improving the tools developed around LibreMarket and related projects.

Each contribution helps better represent the real needs of the community and guide future developments.

> [!TIP]
> A community project evolves thanks to the people who participate in it. If you use the collective's tools, your voice helps shape their future.
