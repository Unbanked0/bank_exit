> [!IMPORTANT]
> It is possible to export the merchants displayed on the map and import them into a mapping application such as [CoMaps](https://www.comaps.app), which works even without an internet connection.

This tutorial walks you through the steps required to access merchant data at any time, whether you are traveling, on a train, or without network access.

{{toc}}

## Export Merchants from the Map

Select your search filters on the map page, or leave them unchanged to export all merchants.

In this example, we will export Monero merchants located in France.

Open the menu by clicking on the three vertical dots, then click **Download merchants**.

![Export merchants as GPX](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/en/00_export_merchants_as_gpx.png") %>)

## Open CoMaps

Open the **CoMaps** application on your smartphone.

![CoMaps logo](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/logo_comaps.webp") %>)

## Open Bookmarks and Tracks

From the map view, click on the **star icon** in the bottom menu to access the **Bookmarks and tracks** screen.

![Empty map](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/en/01_empty_map.png") %>)

## Import the GPX File

In the options menu, select **Import bookmarks and tracks**.

![Import GPX settings](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/en/02_settings_import_gpx.png") %>)

Browse to the `.gpx` file you just downloaded (usually located in your device's **Downloads** folder) and select it. The import process will start automatically.

> [!NOTE]
> Depending on the number of merchants contained in the file, the import process may take more or less time.

## View Imported Merchants

Once imported, merchants will appear on the map.

You can now view or edit them for offline navigation.

![Map with opened marker](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/en/04_maps_with_opened_marker.png") %>)

## Hide or Show Imported Merchants

To hide the newly imported merchants, click again on the **star icon** at the bottom of the map and uncheck the small green eye icon on the left side of the imported file entry.

Click the eye icon again to display them.

![Imported GPX settings](<%= image_path("tutorials/import-merchants-in-comaps/en/03_settings_gpx_imported.png") %>)

> [!WARNING]
> There is no automatic synchronization. To update the merchant data, you will need to download the merchants again and import them into CoMaps manually.
