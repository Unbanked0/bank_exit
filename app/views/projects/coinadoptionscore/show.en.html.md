> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CoinAdoptionScore** is an analytics project that aims to measure the real-world use of cryptocurrencies. Instead of focusing only on price or market capitalization, it looks in particular at the number of merchants that accept them as a means of payment.

Today, thousands of cryptocurrencies exist and are often compared mainly by their market value. But one essential question remains difficult to measure: **which ones are actually used in everyday life?**

The [CoinAdoptionScore](https://coinadoptionscore.com) project provides an indicator that helps observe the commercial adoption of different digital currencies and compare their real-world usage with their financial valuation.

[![CoinAdoptionScore illustration](<%= image_path('projects/coinadoptionscore.jpg') %>)](https://coinadoptionscore.com)

## Why measure real adoption?

The price of a cryptocurrency does not necessarily reflect its actual use.

A currency may have a high market value while being rarely used to purchase goods and services. Conversely, a less well-known currency may have a stronger presence in everyday transactions.

**CoinAdoptionScore** aims to provide another perspective:

- How many businesses accept this currency?
- How is it actually used?
- Does its adoption match its current market valuation?

## How does the score work?

The **CoinAdoptionScore** principle is based on a comparison between:

- the number of merchants accepting a cryptocurrency;
- its market capitalization.

The goal is to create a relative adoption indicator: a currency with many accepting merchants compared to its market value receives a higher score.

The data used comes in particular from open sources such as [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) for merchant listings.

## What does this tool provide?

CoinAdoptionScore allows users to:

- compare different cryptocurrencies based on their commercial usage;
- track the evolution of their adoption;
- distinguish real-world usage from pure speculation;
- highlight projects that are used as actual means of exchange.

The goal is to provide additional information alongside traditional financial indicators.

## A different perspective on cryptocurrencies

Cryptocurrencies are often evaluated only through:

- their price;
- their market capitalization;
- their trading volume.

But a currency is also a tool for exchange.

For the **Bank-Exit collective**, adoption by users and merchants is an essential factor: a currency reaches its full purpose when it can be used concretely in everyday life.

## A project connected to the real economy

CoinAdoptionScore is part of a broader effort to encourage the practical use of digital currencies.

Alongside the Collective's [merchant map](<%= maps_path %>), this tool helps provide a better understanding of how cryptocurrency payment ecosystems are developing.

It highlights the people and businesses building an alternative economy based on direct exchanges between users and professionals.
